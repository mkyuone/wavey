AudioNavigator Privacy Policy

AudioNavigator is designed to process audio files locally in your browser.

Data collection

AudioNavigator does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Audio files

Audio files you open in AudioNavigator stay on your device. They are not uploaded to a server.

Tracking

AudioNavigator does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, or third-party tracking.

Permissions

The Chrome extension does not request access to websites or browsing data.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
